Overview

An interactive analytics dashboard designed to track artwork performance, visitor engagement, and platform activity through clear and actionable visual insights.

Purpose

  • Monitor artwork views and QR scan performance.
  • Analyze artist engagement and visitor behavior.
  • Track audience reach across different cities.
  • Support data-driven decisions for content and engagement growth.

Design perspective

  • Clean and minimalist layout focused on readability.
  • KPI cards provide instant performance snapshots.
  • Balanced use of trend lines, bar charts, and donut charts for quick analysis.
  • Warm color palette and structured sections enhance the user experience.

The palette borrows from canvas, ink and saffron: warm neutrals let the data lead, and one terracotta carries emphasis.

Conclusion

Katha Canvas transforms engagement and artwork data into meaningful insights, enabling stakeholders to understand audience interactions, measure performance, and identify growth opportunities at a glance.
