Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze logistics operations, shipment performance, delivery trends, and transportation efficiency through clear and meaningful visual insights.

Purpose

  • Monitor key logistics and shipment performance indicators.
  • Analyze delivery patterns and operational trends.
  • Track logistics performance to identify potential inefficiencies.
  • Support data-driven decisions for improving operational visibility and efficiency.

Design perspective

  • Clean, structured layout for easy interpretation of logistics data.
  • KPI cards provide a quick overview of operational performance.
  • Interactive charts and visualizations explore shipment trends and logistics metrics.
  • Organized sections and a user-friendly interface support efficient data exploration.

The palette borrows from signage and maps: cool neutrals let the data lead, a deep teal carries structure, and amber flags what needs attention.

Conclusion

The Logistics Analytics Dashboard transforms operational data into actionable insights, helping stakeholders monitor logistics performance, understand shipment trends, and identify opportunities to improve operational efficiency and decision-making.
