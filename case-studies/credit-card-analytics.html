Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze credit card customer behavior, spending patterns, transaction activity, and financial performance through data-driven visual insights.

Purpose

  • Monitor credit card transactions and overall spending activity.
  • Analyze customer usage patterns and spending behavior.
  • Track key financial metrics to understand business performance.
  • Support data-driven decision-making through interactive reporting.

Design perspective

  • Clean and structured layout for easy interpretation of financial data.
  • KPI cards to highlight important performance indicators.
  • Interactive charts and visualizations to explore transaction trends and customer patterns.
  • Organized sections and intuitive navigation to improve the user experience.

Conclusion

The Credit Card Analytics Dashboard transforms transaction and customer data into meaningful insights, helping stakeholders understand spending behavior, monitor financial performance, and identify patterns that support informed business decisions.
